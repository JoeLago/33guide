## 1. Basic formula — Attack and Relics

$$
\underbrace{\text{BaseDmg}}_{97.5 \;\times\; \text{Ratio} \;\times\; \text{WeapUpgrade}} \times \overbrace{(1 + 0.02 \times \text{Atk})}^{\text{Each atk point is +2\% dmg}} \times \left(1 + \sum \text{Relic\%}\right)
$$

This is the simple damage formula for "vanilla" damage (no costrike, no status effect, etc.). **BaseDmg** averages 97.5 (100 max, 95 min), multiplied by a ratio specific to the attack you are using (from 0.4 for a recalled light bow arrow at 39 dmg, up to 1.9 for the glaive last attack of a combo at 185 dmg).

Then it's multiplied by another ratio that depends on your weapon upgrade(s). For Bow with <Pill slug="small-quiver" />, that's 1.5. Upgrades are multiplicative with each other — adding <Pill slug="double-shot" /> on top of <Pill slug="small-quiver" /> gives $1.5 \times 0.75 = 1.125$. Per-attack and per-upgrade ratios are listed on each weapon's intro page.

Next, we multiply by a stat term: `(1 + 0.02 · atk)` — each attack point adds +2% damage.

Finally, relics. Relics and most perks are **additive** between each other — hence the Σ. Example: <Pill slug="thorn-of-the-martyr" /> (+15%) and <Pill slug="cocytus-abiding-ice" /> (+25%) give +40% total, not +43.75%:

$$1 + \sum\text{Relic\%} = 1 + 0.15 + 0.25 = 1.4$$

Your damage will almost never be exactly the same in identical conditions because there's a ±2.5% window around the calculated value (only exception: daggers' Takedown).

### Full example

Playing daggers, light attacks (0.8 ratio), <Pill slug="stabs" /> upgrade (×1.2 light damage), 18 atk, relics <Pill slug="cruel-harpy-feather" /> (0.25 after blessing) and <Pill slug="cocytus-abiding-ice" /> (0.25):

$$
97.5 \times 0.8 \times 1.2 \;\times\; (1 + 0.02 \times 18) \;\times\; (1 + 0.25 + 0.25) \;=\; 93.6 \times 1.36 \times 1.50 \;\cong\; 190.94
$$

With the damage window, the hit will be between roughly 186 and 196.

From here on, we call **additional damage** every +% added alongside relics% (additive), and call out anything **multiplicative** separately. This distinction is crucial for the diminishing-returns section.

$$
\text{BaseDmg} \times (1 + 0.02 \times \text{Atk}) \times \left(1 + \sum\text{Relic\%} \;\overbrace{\boxed{{\color{gray}+\;\ldots}}}^{\color{red}\text{additional damage}}\right) \underbrace{\boxed{{\color{gray}\times\;\ldots}}}_{\color{red}\text{multiplicative damage}}
$$

## 2. Advanced formula — Costrikes and Critical hits

Co-strike additional damage scales on empathy (+1% per empathy point) and is **additive with relics**.

Critical hits add +100% additional damage (and more with additional crit damage). Also **additive with relics**.

## 3. Status effects, Armor, String of Jibril, Dash combos

<Figure src="/assets/figures/damage/formula-final.png" alt="Full final damage formula including status, armor, dash combo, and atk-multiplier terms" variant="wide" />

### Status effects

- **Armor Break** — once armor is broken with enough heavy damage. Prevents the target from doing any action for 3 seconds.
- **Vulnerable** — built up by any damage while <Pill slug="yawning-maw-of-truth" /> is equipped. Multiplies damage taken by ×2.25 for 5s.
- **Exposed** — built up by light damage while <Pill slug="sacrosant-vernicle" /> is equipped. ×1.4 damage taken for 5s.
- **Marked** — built up by costrike damage while <Pill slug="soiled-purificator" /> is equipped. +100% costrike damage taken for 5s.
- **Weak** — built up by any damage while <Pill slug="oxidized-flagon" /> is equipped. Target deals less damage to players for 5s.

Vulnerability and Exposure are **additive to each other**; an enemy under both takes +165% more damage (×2.65).

Status effects only trigger once a threshold of "status damage" has been done — depends on the target's max HP and type:

| Target | Exposed / Marked / Vulnerable | Weak |
|--------|-------------------------------|------|
| Normal | 33% of max HP | 16.5% of max HP |
| Elite | 20% of max HP | 10% of max HP |
| Boss | 20% of a bar (36,000 HP) / 2% max HP | 10% of a bar (18,000 HP) / 1% max HP |

Weak doesn't directly add damage, but it's the easiest status to trigger <Pill slug="tusk-of-ciriatto" /> on (twice easier than Exposed/Marked).

### Armor

Armor reduces light damage by 60% (×0.4). Killing an armored enemy with light damage alone requires 2.5× its max HP.

Example: an Arciere has 950 HP, so you'd need to deal 950 ÷ 0.4 = 2,375 light damage ($950 / 0.4 = 950 \times 2.5 = 2375$) to kill it through armor.

Regular enemies have 40–60% of their HP as armor *(Arciere itself has 600)*; elites have ~35–40%. Switch to heavy on elites — with small exceptions for full Coop-Power builds or running <Pill slug="blessed-chains" /> / <Pill slug="sacrosant-vernicle" />.

### Dash Combos

Performed by dashing and inputting an attack within ~20 frames (0.3 s) during the dash. Adds ×1.2 (+20% multiplicative), ×1.5 with <Pill slug="cosmic-rebirth" />.

Most powerful when your attack speed is low (between 1.2 and 0.8 atk/sec) but damage is high. Sometimes even better when paired with <Pill slug="worn-scapular" />, which speeds up dashes.

### String of Jibril

<Pill slug="string-of-jibril" /> is the only relic that affects the atk multiplier (along with <Pill slug="celestial-green-feather" /> indirectly): it replaces the 0.02 per-atk multiplier with 0.0266, so each attack point becomes worth +2.66% damage — effectively an extra atk point every 3 atk points.

At 23 atk (max without converters) this multiplies damage by ×1.104 (+10.4% overall), comparable to a +50% additional damage relic stacked on top of an existing +400% additional damage. The multiplier grows with attack.

It's a really good relic for any atk build, especially paired with <Pill slug="celestial-green-feather" /> if you can max empathy during the run.

### Agony

3 or 9 ordeals equipped trigger Agony levels. No direct effect on displayed damage, but monsters have more HP for the player(s) running ordeals — effective damage reduction (and changes how %HP status thresholds work). See [Ordeals & Agony](/ordeals.html).

## 4. Diminishing returns — invest in multipliers

The damage formula is a chain of multiplications, but the "additional damage" term (relics + costrike + crit) is a sum. Sums suffer from diminishing returns; multipliers don't.

### What is diminishing returns?

Vanilla bow normal attack, 15 atk, +50% damage relic: $\approx 97.5 \times 0.6 \times 1.3 \times 1.5 \approx 114$.

$$
58.5 \times 1.3 \times 1.5 \;\cong\; 114
$$

Now add a second relic at +25%. If relics were **multiplicative**, you'd expect 114 × 1.25 = 142.5.

$$
58.5 \times 1.3 \times 1.5 \times 1.25 \;\cong\; 142.5
$$

But relics are **additive**, so the real calculation:

$$
58.5 \times 1.3 \times (1 + 0.5 + 0.25) = 58.5 \times 1.3 \times 1.75 \;\cong\; 133
$$

an effective +16.6% overall (not +25%).

This gets worse the higher your existing multiplier. Adding +25% to an already +300% pile is only an effective +6.25% damage.

Once your additional damage stack is already high (with or without crits, depending on play), invest in *other* multipliers:

- **Atk multiplier**: <Pill slug="string-of-jibril" />, <Pill slug="celestial-green-feather" />.
- **Status effects**: triggers <Pill slug="tusk-of-ciriatto" /> and adds team-shared damage.
- **Crit sustain**: <Pill slug="cracked-rosary-beads" />, <Pill slug="clerical-cilice" />.
- **Coop Power relics**, or just **survivability**.
