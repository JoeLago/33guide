The Ordeal system makes the game harder but more rewarding. There are 12 available Ordeals; the number you can equip simultaneously increases each time you defeat a boss while having the maximum equipped.

## Agony thresholds

Once 3, 6, 9, or 12 ordeals are equipped, an Agony effect activates in addition to the equipped ordeals:

| Ordeals equipped | Agony effect |
|------------------|--------------|
| **3** | Descending heals players around for 25% HP, but Inferno and Purgatorio are deadlier. |
| **6** | Relics can be blessed on drop or reroll, but ordeals are more dangerous (gold text in list below). |
| **9** | Monsters can drop weapon upgrades, but all Worlds are deadlier. |
| **12** | Bosses drop one legendary cosmetic on death (5 total per boss), but you cannot enter soul form — one death = 100% shatter. |

## Effective damage reduction by world

"Deadlier" means monsters have more HP *for you* (effective damage reduction) and deal more damage:

| Ordeals equipped | Inferno | Purgatorio | Paradiso* |
|------------------|---------|------------|-----------|
| 3–8 | ×2 | ×1.25 | ×1 |
| 9–12 | ×2.5 | ×1.56 | ×1.25 |

| Ordeals equipped | Inferno | Purgatorio | Paradiso* |
|------------------|---------|------------|-----------|
| 3–8 | ×0.5 | ×0.8 | ×1 |
| 9–12 | ×0.4 | ×0.64 | ×0.8 |

<small>*Paradiso values are theoretical. In Private Session, A&E already have a ×0.64 multiplier (stacks multiplicatively with Agony), but no other enemy nor Lucifer carries any other multiplier.</small>
