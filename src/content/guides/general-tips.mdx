## Always enter chambers with a key

You should have heard this almost everywhere, but it's too important to not repeat. Don't rush a chamber if you have a key, either — help people around you find one too, and start stacking some for later.

If you struggle to find keys, there are [detailed maps](/maps) showing the possible locations of chests — there are only 3 different setups for each map currently. Gather people by using emotes on elite-boss chests (3 per map); they're the main bone source alongside Elite Lucidos.

## Level up

Base stats are very important. At the start of a game you should be able to level up 2–3 times easily before entering a first chamber (max out one stat if you enter at the very start of a session). Empty your dust before entering each subsequent chamber.

## Don't reroll relics early

If you pick up relics you don't particularly want at first, keep them for a while until you have a solid number. You won't obtain the same relic twice, so holding ones you don't want still trims the relic pool (and saves rerolls later). You can do 1–2 early rerolls to get something at least useful, but save bones for later.

## Maintain costrikes for crits

The +100% damage from crits is one of the most important and sometimes hardest parts of dealing damage — especially with the +100% crit damage perk <Pill slug="empyreal-deceptiveness" />. Some builds should take one of the two crit-maintainer common relics: it's not a loss for +200% damage.

## Don't sleep on status effects

The four status effects (Vulnerable, Marked, Exposed, Weak) multiply damage taken or boost specific damage types. Don't sleep on <Pill slug="sacrosant-vernicle" /> (exposure) for light-attack builds, or <Pill slug="soiled-purificator" /> (marking) for costrike-heavy builds.

Exposure multiplies damage taken; Marking boosts costrike damage. These are already strong on their own, and become even better when paired with <Pill slug="tusk-of-ciriatto" /> (+125% additional damage to status-affected enemies) — more damage than a base crit.

## Get every boss relic

[Boss relics](/relics) are game-changers. Always get them all: complete all ascensions and the secret chamber. A big chunk of the boss damage shown in this guide is thanks to these relics.

## Break Elite Armor first with heavy attacks

Armor makes elites have ~2.5× more effective HP (60% light damage reduction). Armor itself only equates to ~40% of HP, so breaking it is way more efficient than forcekilling through. Killing an enemy without breaking armor first renders all heavy damage to it null and void.

Exceptions: builds running <Pill slug="blessed-chains" /> or <Pill slug="sacrosant-vernicle" />, or full Coop-Power-oriented builds.
