## First look at crossbows

High range, high damage single-target weapon with no cleave at base. Its ability launches a bolt that marks (brand) a target, making them suffer x2 damage from your crossbow, which will be necessary to keep a good dps. Fortunately, the brand ability allows us to cut a lot of animation frames, so we'll be using it constantly in order to maximize attack speed and damage. Hitting a branded target generates 3 Pride (max 20), which is consumed by heavy attacking (1 heavy bolt per Pride). Note that performing a Dash Combo on a heavy attack applies the Dash Combo bonus on every shot, and since DC is a multiplicative buff, this is an easy way to make damage ramp up. Do not forget to equip <Pill slug="cosmic-rebirth" /> perk for more effect!

<Coop weapon="crossbows" />

Its Coop Power Ballista creates a turret that launches 5 pairs* of single-target bolts per second, each dealing 300 heavy damage at max level (3000 dps) for 11 seconds, with duration scaling on empathy (very slightly, haven't seen it last more than 16s even at 35+ emp with candle for both), and it cannot deal costrikes. Cannot apply any status effect. *both bolts hitting the same enemy will show the damage only once, where it in fact happens twice.

**Base stats (no brand)**

- Attack speed: 1.4 2-bolts combo + brand per sec (see tips and tricks). 24 frames to launch 2 bolts + 18 frames brand
- Light damage: 40.6 x2
- Heavy damage: 58.5 first bolt, then 31 for any subsequent bolt
- Charged attack rate: 0.43s charging then 6 shots per sec (26 frames charging + 10 frames per subsequent shot + 16 frames animation end)
- Brand ability damage: 49

## Tips and tricks

**How to Quick Shot**

Light attack ending animation being 34 frames, and brand ability being only 18 frames with no cooldown, we can use the ability at the very end of the second shot to gain several frames, making the animation around 25% quicker, plus having more damage from using the ability, plus not needing to remind to brand the enemy being attacked. Only downside being that the brand is imprecise and sometimes goes off, and could hit another enemy because of the weird auto-aim the game has.

<img class="demo-gif" src="/assets/gifs/crossbows-quick-shot.gif" alt="Quick Shot brand cancel demo" loading="lazy" />

**How to Skipping Bolts**

<Pill slug="skipping-bolts" /> usage is quite weird: since hitboxes are generous, bolts will almost always go on a second enemy that is right behind, however if the enemy is a bit far you'll usually see the bolt skipping in plain void. As a rule of thumb we can consider that a bolt will always ricochet in a 140° fashion depending on whether you hit the top of the hitbox or the bottom. This way we can more consistently hit farther enemies, at the price of being placed well. This will sometimes still be very random because of the auto aim.

<img class="demo-gif" src="/assets/gifs/crossbows-skipping-bolts.gif" alt="Skipping Bolts ricochet demo" loading="lazy" />

**Flurry of Bolts Pride generation**

This is not always exact and sometimes bolts from the second shot will generate one more Pride for some reason, but here's a general rule:

- 1 bolt hitting generates 1 pride
- 2 bolts hitting generates 3 pride
- 3 bolts hitting generates 4 pride
- 4 bolts hitting generates 6 pride

As long as you are close to the enemy, you're going to generate more Pride than usual, up to twice more.

<Build id="flurry-of-bolts-showtime" name="Prideful" upgrades={["flurry-of-bolts", "showtime"]} desc="Heavy attack with Flurry to charge Pride; Showtime for damage.">

In this build, we use <Pill slug="flurry-of-bolts" /> to quickly charge up 20 Pride and <Pill slug="showtime" /> (which ends up with 100% uptime), also making it a really good build for costrikes per second (even in counter!) because of the heavy high attack rate, this is the go-to build to play with <Pill slug="muddy-apostle-medal" /> and for Coop Power spamming (reminder that Ballista is 3000 stable dps and barely scales on empathy).

Note that <Pill slug="showtime" /> being additional damage, the shown damage numbers below will not scale properly when you get more additional damage. For this reason I'll also write the numbers at +300% additional damage already (which is crit with <Pill slug="empyreal-deceptiveness" /> + empathy boost from costrikes + some relics or Marked status effect), at this point it is effectively a x1.075 damage multiplier (+7.5% damage).

Conversely, costrikes/crit not being achieved on every hit of <Pill slug="flurry-of-bolts" />, <Pill slug="showtime" /> helps reduce the slight gap of damage caused by this (Light attack damage is approximately only 25% of all the damage done with the gameplay loop).

The optimal gameplay loop is: Brand > Light > Brand > Light > Dash Combo Heavy 20 bolts. However since <Pill slug="flurry-of-bolts" /> requires to be melee and dashing being a large movement and all attacks having a small recoil, this would be only achievable against a wall (or left/right on large hitboxes), so we'll add a second dash to get back to melee for the general case: Brand > Dash Combo Light > Brand > Light > Dash Combo Heavy 20 bolts

<Build id="flurry-of-bolts-explosive-brands" name="REAL SHOTGUN" upgrades={["flurry-of-bolts", "explosive-brands"]} desc="HEHE EXPLOSIONS.">

THEY INVENTED GUNPOWDER FR. Quite easy to play build, that is theoretically the best dps in the game, however ends up being random in terms of damage when it comes to costrikes and critical hits.

Concept is simple: get to melee, then spam the light + brand combo, making the brand explode in big damage at every light attack.

It's first downside is that it is almost unable to get damage from heavy attack (or doing so would be dropping quite a lot of damage), making the <Pill slug="blessed-chains" /> almost mandatory (useless on boss though, especially if your team can easily handle elites). Finally, the second shot from the light attack may not benefit from the x2 damage from the brand if it exploded after the first shot (which also is random, all 4 bolts hitting doesn't necessarily causes the explosion to proc), hence the damage range in the stats below.

<Build id="explosive-brands-showtime" name="skill issue?" upgrades={["explosive-brands", "showtime"]} desc="Theoretical, extremely skilled, mostly not worth the risk.">

Mostly a theoretical build, as it requires extreme precision in every input (and low ping probably, as sometimes Pride generation and brand stacks seem to be skipped, causing the rotation time to expand, losing dps). Here we'll be using <Pill slug="explosive-brands" /> + <Pill slug="showtime" /> + Dash Combo 4 heavy spam with <Pill slug="cosmic-rebirth" /> in order to try to maximize single target dps without any costrike loss (in best case scenario only, unfortunately). The Dash Combo 4 heavy is able to proc the explosion as quick as light attacks does (without <Pill slug="flurry-of-bolts" /> ofc), while dealing quite more damage due to the first bolt being stronger and all bolts benefiting from Dash Combo.

The rotation will be as follow (we'll suppose we start with 20 pride): (Brand -> Dash Combo Heavy 4 bolts) x3 -> (Brand -> Light) x2

The 3 Dash Combo Heavy will cost 12 pride, just as much as the 2 Light should replenish, allowing for <Pill slug="showtime" /> to be always up. However, a lot of things can be messed during the rotation:

- Releasing one too much bolt during a heavy attack will make the explosion unable to costrike/crit with a high probability
- Releasing one too much bolt during a heavy attack will cost more pride than light attacks will regenerate, requiring one more on the next chain, but a single one won't trigger the explosion, leaving it at 2 stacks and disabling the next heavy from triggering a costrike explosion (unless using a 2-bullets heavy), so it may be better to compensate with another second light attack
- Holding back one too much bolt during a heavy attack chain will leave the Brand stacks at 3, losing one explosion and making the next one unable to costrike (unless using a 1-bullet heavy)
- Sometimes the quick light/brand combo drifts off, causing the target to not get an additional brand stack, and preventing Pride regeneration, thus making the whole rotation to drift into one or two more light attacks

Since this build uses <Pill slug="showtime" />, which provides additional damage, the numbers will also be shown when at already +300% additional damage from other sources (<Pill slug="showtime" /> being x1.075 damage at this point).
