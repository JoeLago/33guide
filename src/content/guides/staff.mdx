## First look at staff

Staff is a highly supportive weapon, able to slow or immobilize groups of enemies, while being able to output a good amount of damage (and also lots of costrikes).

<Coop weapon="staff" />

Staff coop power creates a huge rectangular area of purple gas that lasts 16s long at max level (duration scaling on empathy), immobilizing enemies within it (it also deals like 10 damage on activation and interrupts enemies within it at the time). Paired with torpor that reduces the movement speed of enemies for up to 10 seconds (depending on your sloth gauge), staff has a great control of the field and provides great survivability to the team, a well placed power during ascension can guarantee its quick and safe success.

**Base stats**

- Attack speed: 2 orbs per sec
- Base damage: 73.125 (x0.75 ratio)
- Charged attack damage: 102.375 x2 (x1.05 ratio)
- Charging time: 1s (0.55s charge, 0.45s animation)
- Torpor damage: 126.75 (x1.3 ratio)

## Tips and tricks

Staff light attacks without them being upgraded (i.e. without <Pill slug="scattering-orbs" />) are… Terrible. Charged attacks fire 2 orbs per second which is the same rate as light attacks, but with twice costrikes (since heavy damage generated 2 costrikes to the counter), and damage is also higher than light attack. You may try to cast charged attacks as much as you can, except if you play with <Pill slug="scattering-orbs" /> or need the extra mobility that light attack offers over charged attacks.

Torpor cast being almost instant, it is always good to use it sometimes to provide more support, as long as it doesn't break any combo (as in <Pill slug="scattering-orbs" />). It's also a good tool to save your costrike counter quickly.

<Build id="torpor-wave-lethargy" name="Medusa" upgrades={["torpor-wave", "lethargy"]} desc="Mixed support + AoE damage with immobilization.">

Who needs staff coop power when your wave does the same thing? Here you can either use 4x light attacks then torpor, or 2x heavy attacks then torpor. Heavy offers more charge in some situations since it's an aoe and also more damage, while light offers more mobility and are the same type of damage as your wave (good if you have +50% light damage relic). This little combo will be enough to hold your enemies in place since torpor status' stacks, and you may charge your torpor quicker than they lose it.

If you want more costrikes and damage, you can replace <Pill slug="lethargy" /> with <Pill slug="bouncing-orbs" />, this will also allow you to get more strikes to charge your torpor quicker and be able to slow even more enemies.

### Build info

- **Attack speed:** 2.5 hits per sec
- **Damage:** 190.1 torpor wave
- **Core dps:** 241.3 (light) | 299.8 (heavy)
- **Costrike per second:** 2.5 (4.5 in counter for heavy attacks)
- **Cleaving capacity:** High
- **Difficulty rating:** Easy
- **Best fitting build archetype:** Mixed, aoe damage + support with Lethargy and coop power

### Pros

- Great support by constantly immobilizing enemies around
- Still correct damage for the support it offers
- Can go full light damage

### Cons

- Quite low costrike count
- No input buffer for torpor, annoying to play with heavy attacks

</Build>
