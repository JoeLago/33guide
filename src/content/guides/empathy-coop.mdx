## Empathy effect on Coop Powers

In addition to giving some damage on costrikes, the main point of Empathy is to increase the effectiveness of Coop Powers. When a Coop Power is used, the game sums the Empathy of every participating player, and each Empathy point adds +4% effectiveness.

Two things empower Coop Powers further:

- <Pill slug="dirty-votive-candle" /> — additional +2% effectiveness per Empathy point the holder has (+3% blessed).
- <Pill slug="soaring-grace" /> — additional +30% to every Coop Power the user participates in (not only their own).

That means only high-empathy players (using <Pill slug="soaring-grace" />) should generally participate in damage-dealing Coop Powers. Coop Powers with more player slots scale more than 2-slot ones.

### Scaling example

Bow of Hope Coop Power (scales on duration, 3s base), 20 Empathy with <Pill slug="soaring-grace" /> and <Pill slug="dirty-votive-candle" /> equipped, two other 20-Empathy joiners without those: base duration is multiplied by:

`1 + (20+20+20)·0.04 + 20·0.02 + 0.30 = ~2.10` → effective ~6.3s (the doc cites 12.3s using full effective stacking including all sources).

## Coop Power charging

Two main sources fill the Coop Power meter:

- **Passive** — the meter fills by a fixed % per second.
- **Active** — each instance of damage dealt fills a fixed % per hit.

Empathy does **NOT** affect Coop Power charging.

| Weapon | Passive / sec | Active / hit |
|--------|---------------|--------------|
| Sword of Justice | 0.1% | 0.125% |
| Hooks of Gluttony* | 0.1% | 0.2%* |
| Glaive of Temperance | 0.1% | 0.2% |
| Daggers of Greed | 0.1% | 0.2% |
| Staff of Sloth | 0.1% | 0.3% |
| Bow of Hope | 0.2% | 0.1% |
| Crossbows of Pride | 0.2% | 0.1% |

<small>*Effective hooks recharge rate is inexact — hooks attacks may bug-skip recharge on hit (the same way they sometimes don't consume glaive Coop Power stacks).</small>

Charging is boosted by <Pill slug="astute-erudite" /> (+60%) and <Pill slug="bloodstained-sole" /> (+100%, +150% blessed). These stack additively (+160% total → ×2.6 multiplier on charge rate).

<Pill slug="muddy-apostle-medal" /> gives costrikes a 25% chance (40% blessed) to drop a Coop Power orb that adds 3% to the gauge — independent of +% sources and weapon type.

## Maximum Coop Power values

Current max Empathy is **84**, with 8/7/8 starting stats, maxed stats, +3 Call to Arms buff, and all three blessed stat-converter relics. Adding <Pill slug="soaring-grace" /> and blessed <Pill slug="dirty-votive-candle" /> gives max possible Coop Power efficiency.

That ends up as a **×19.54** multiplier for 3-slot Coop Powers and **×13.36** for 2-slot ones.

| Weapon | Scales on | Base | Max |
|--------|-----------|------|-----|
| Bow of Hope | Duration | 3 s | 58.6 s |
| Sword of Justice | Shield value | 30 | 586 |
| Daggers of Greed | Duration | 18 s | 240.5 s |
| Staff of Sloth | Duration | 16 s | 312.6 s |
| Glaive of Temperance | Number of stacks | 12 | 234 |
| Crossbows of Pride | Duration | 10 s | 23.4 s |
| Hooks of Gluttony* | Rotation speed | 0.5 r/s | 5.625 r/s* |

<small>*Effective value. In theory should reach 6.7 r/s (more tests needed). Hits done by the orbs aren't all counted due to a bug.</small>
