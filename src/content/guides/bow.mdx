## First look at bow

One of Bow main strength is the number of times it can hit per second at a range, providing a quick costrike buildup, allowing others to get costrikes easily, and charging coop power faster. On the other hand, heavy attacks provide good damage, at a higher range than light attacks, with additional cleave. Note that multiple arrows recalled at the same time will only be able to deal a single costrike on each enemy!

<Coop weapon="bow" />

Bow coop power unleashes a volley of 3 arrows in 3 columns 2.4 times per seconds, it lasts longer with empathy, and each arrow deals a fixed 300 light damage (when upgraded to max). This does a whooping max of 21.6 hits per second, for a max 6480 dps on multiple targets (lucifer large hitbox allow for 5 out of 9 arrows to hit) and the total damage it does only scales on empathy. Arrows from Coop Power can apply status effects (Marked requires YMOT).

**Base stats**

- Fire rate: 2.5 arrows per sec
- Arrow damage: 58.5 (×0.6 ratio)
- Recalled arrow damage: 39 (×0.4 ratio)
- Arrow charging time: 1.05s (0.5s charging + 0.55s firing animation)
- Charged arrow damage: 146.25 (×1.5 ratio)
- Recalled charged arrow damage: 107.25 (×1.1 ratio)
- Guiding Light duration: 0.58s
- Guiding Light with animation cancel: ~0.2s (see tips and tricks)

Note: Performing a Dash Combo then instantly recalling the arrow(s) shot will also apply Dash Combo multiplier to recalled arrows. This was reported a while back and it's unclear if it's intended, so where DC is used with instant recall the calculation is shown in parenthesis with the effective value in red.

## Tips and tricks

If you're running a light only bow build, you'll be having a lot of mobility since firing an arrow doesn't lock you in place for long, plus if you run auto recall you won't be affected by guiding light speed debuff. In this case, it is good to aim for empathy/coop power build, so you can also help others activate their power.

If you're running a heavy attack build, you'll notice that guiding light can feel quite long (and heavy arrows cannot be recalled by auto recall). But worry not! You can actually cancel its animation by instantly dodging out of it, just beware if you have an arrow planted far away, you may wait some frames for it to come back before cancelling. Note that instantly attacking after dashing will proc a Dash Combo, making <Pill slug="cosmic-rebirth" /> very good for such builds, and <Pill slug="worn-scapular" /> allows to perform dash cancels more often, effectively increasing attack speed.

This can be done in a short window of 12 frames (counting dodge short frames too), yet better aim for 15 for all arrows to come back. Of course this means you may not be able to dodge when you want to, so you'll need to anticipate your dodge direction!

Note that guiding light can also cancel the end of the animation of firing an arrow, reducing the heavy firing animation down to 8 frames! Making firing an arrow time: 30 frames charging + 8 frames animation + 15 frames cancelling = 0.89s, which unfortunately is quicker than dash recovery time, unless… (no spoiler). Because of this, if the previously shooted arrow is an heavy, guiding light will be counted as having a 0.17s duration.
