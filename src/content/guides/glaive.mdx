## First look at glaive

Glaive has a good cleave, especially with its heavy attack. However it requires good placement, else it suffers from 90% damage reduction (x0.1 multiplier) if the enemy is too close. Fortunately, placement is made more easy with the backstep ability (which is also an iframe, making glaive able to totally ignore dash). Also note that it just requires for 1 pixel of the enemy hitbox to be in the max damage area for attacks to have normal damage, making it easier on elites and bosses.

Each attack done with glaive consumes 1 Temperance, which can be recovered using backstep (3 per backstep, max 6 Temperance stored). In addition, having 4 or more Temperance causes the attacks to deal x1.15 (+15%) damage, and having 0 Temperance makes them deal x0.1 damage. Thus we'll need to use backstep constantly between each combo ideally. Backstep can be used once every ~0.5 seconds (28 frames to be exact) or twice in a quick succession, which will put it in a 0.5s cooldown AFTER the backstep.

<Coop weapon="glaive" />

Its Coop Power Aftershock gives 12 base stacks (scaling on participants' empathy), each successful attack (swing or projectile) consumes a single stack, even if multiple enemies are hit, dealing 250 heavy damage to each of those enemies at max level. When there are multiple enemies, it is best for high cleave builds to take this power for best total damage output. The hits done by Aftershock CAN costrike and WILL costrike if it is one, bypassing the "1 costrike at a time" limit that usually prevent two instances of damage at the same time to both be costrikes. Cannot apply any status effect. (thanks Romallka for confirming this) Aftershock optimization in tips and tricks.

**Base stats** (without Temperance x1.15 dmg buff)

- Attack speed: 1.8 attack per sec in average for continuous light attacks and perfect backstep (backstep is 18 frames*, as much as 3rd atk ending frames)
- Light combo: 126.75 → 146.26 → 185.25 (x1.3 → 1.5 → 1.9 ratio)
- Heavy combo: 146.25 → 185.25 (x1.5 → 1.9 ratio)
- Charging time: 0.93s (26 frames charging, 30 frames animation)

*we'll give 4 additional frames for giving a direction to backstep, which is almost always different as attack direction, putting attack speed at 1.73 with bs cancel on 3rd attack, and charging time to 0.8s with cancel + we do not consider Temperance dmg buff here, but it will be accounted in every build

## Tips and tricks

**Aftershock optimization**

- Bow Coop Power is able to proc it, and sometimes does not consume any stack by doing so (it seems that enemies already "inside" at activation won't consume any)
- <Pill slug="preserved-heretic-head" /> is able to proc it at no cost (won't costrike either)
- Staff <Pill slug="scattering-orbs" /> and <Pill slug="bouncing-orbs" /> does not seem to consume multiple stacks anymore
- Most of Hooks attacks won't consume stacks (bug)

**Where to backstep, general case**

Step 1: identify the two possible places where you'll be at the same distance from the enemy after backstepping (black lines showing distance from enemy, blue arrows showing backstep distance)

Step 2: identify the opposite direction from backstep directions (green dots)

Step 3: ideally, wait for your 3 light combo or 2 heavy combo to end then immediately direct your cursor/joystick in the wanted direction (the quicker the more dps)

Step 4: backstep to step 1

Note that hitboxes are quite generous, no need to be 100% accurate, especially on elites and bosses.

**Easy backstep**

Luckily there are other easy ways to recover Temperance without having to be under pressure every time. The first one consists of totally emptying your Temperance, then backstep in the same direction you're attacking before backstepping in the opposite direction. This however will cost 7.5% dps from attacking at 3 or less Temperance, and will put Backstep in cooldown.

<Build id="master-of-flow-thousand-cuts" name="The Lawnmower" upgrades={["master-of-flow", "thousand-cuts"]} desc="Easiest glaive build, light-attack focused — best for Coop Power spamming.">

Easiest glaive build since everything you have to do is keeping the right distance from the enemy, without being scared of losing too much Temperance while using light attacks. Backstep will be your main tool for quick repositioning still.

This build is also the one that takes most advantage from <Pill slug="thousand-cuts" />, since backstepping to recover Temperance slightly diminish overall light attack speed, making it the best choice for going Coop Power too.

### Build info

- **Attack speed:** 2.2 attack per sec with continuous light attacks (1.28 aps with 2 heavy + backstep)
- **Damage:** 175.66 avg light / 190.61 avg heavy
- **Core dps:** 386.5 full light / 244 full heavy
- **Costrike per second:** 2.2 light / 1.28 heavy
- **Costrike per second in counter:** 2.2 light / 2.56 heavy
- **Cleaving capacity:** Medium with light / High with heavy
- **Difficulty rating:** Easy
- **Best fitting build archetype:** Coop Power + Damage

### Pros

- Best costrike per second rate achievable with glaive
- Easy playstyle
- Backstep can be used as a free second dodge

### Cons

- Quite far from being the most damage achievable with glaive
- Switching to heavy will require to change playstyle at a dps loss

</Build>

<Build id="optimal-glaive-9-ordeals" name="Optimal Glaive — 9 Ordeals" upgrades={[]} desc="Two efficient playstyles when running all 9 ordeals: light-only, or backstep + charged heavy.">

This will allow two main efficient playstyles:

- Light attacks only, with <Pill slug="master-of-flow" /> + <Pill slug="thousand-cuts" /> + <Pill slug="unceasing-blows" />
- Backstep + 1s charged attack, with <Pill slug="one-motion" /> + <Pill slug="deliberation" /> + <Pill slug="unceasing-blows" />

(along with Backstep + heavy for Costrike counter building)
